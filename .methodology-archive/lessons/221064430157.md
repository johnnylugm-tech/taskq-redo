**Failure:** Gate 1 blocked [verify_target]: `make verify-system` does not verify the delivered system
**Fix:** Edit the `verify-system` recipe in the project Makefile. It must invoke the delivered entry point — the program a user would run (`python -m <your_package> …`, your console script, your service) — and that step must be able to fail: no `|| true`, no leading `-`, no `--exit-zero`. A target that chains `test lint coverage` re-runs dimensions this gate already scored and executes none of the system. Do NOT lower any dimension score to express this, and do not re-run the gate before editing the recipe — the recipe, not the run, is what failed.
